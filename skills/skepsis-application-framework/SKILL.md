This skill provides a class‑level, reusable process for applying the **SKEPSIS** (Three‑Layer Interrogation Framework) to strategic transformation plans such as the Metrojaya turnaround. It replaces ad‑hoc questioning with a repeatable, user‑centred workflow that can be invoked from any future session.

## When to Invoke
- Transformation planning (e.g., “Metrojaya turnaround”, “Retail‑media monetisation”).
- Strategic hypothesis testing before committing resources.
- Any user request that asks for deeper analysis of assumptions, stakeholder models, or risk trade‑offs.

## Core Flow (L0 → L3)
1. **L0 – Socratic Destruction** – List and actively dismantle core assumptions.  
2. **L1 – Mental‑Model Mapping** – Capture stakeholder mental models and their implications.  
3. **L2 – Debate Mapping** – List pros/cons for each recommendation; highlight risk concentration.  
4. **L3 – Non‑Obvious Expansion** – Brainstorm high‑leverage, low‑capital expansions; score and prioritize.  
5. **L4 – Synthesis & Review** – Consolidate outputs, run a steering‑committee checkpoint, and capture decisions.

## Reference Files (auto‑generated)
- `references/destruction-<topic>.md` – assumption‑destruction notes.  
- `references/mental-models-<topic>.md` – stakeholder model table.  
- `references/debate-<topic>.md` – pros/cons matrix.  
- `references/expansions-<topic>.md` – expansion ideas with scoring.  
- `plans/<topic>-implementation.md` – actionable roadmap with owners, milestones, KPIs.

### L0 – Destruction
- Assumption: “Influencer sales will clear aging inventory quickly.” → Destruction: “Older, price‑sensitive shoppers may not convert; risk of low ROI.”

### L1 – Mental Models
| Stakeholder | Mental Model | Implication |
|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| Family Owners | Preserve legacy, retain 100% control | No external capital allowed. |
| Store Managers | Limited staff, focus on day‑to‑day | New processes must be net‑zero labor. |
| Brand Shoppers | Value‑price driven, low‑friction shopping | Influencer content must integrate seamlessly. |
| Financial Team | Cost‑efficiency & debt‑reduction priority | All initiatives must be cash‑flow positive within 18 months. |

### L2 – Debate Mapping
| Recommendation | Pros | Cons |
|----------------|------|------|
| BOPIS / Omni‑channel | Captures online demand; leverages existing real‑estate | Requires staffing, may cannibalise footfall if pricing is off |
| Influencer‑Led Brand Marketing | Access to younger audience; fast content turnover | Audience mismatch; dependency on third‑party platforms; content cost may exceed revenue |
| Flash‑Clearance Bundles | Immediate cash inflow; clears shelf space quickly | Potential brand de‑valuation; could accelerate price‑sensitivity backlash |
| Retail‑Media Monétisation (Screen Ads) | Leverages existing digital screens for new revenue | Must acquire advertisers; could clutter experience; revenue uncertain until metrics built |

### L3 – Expansions (High‑Leverage, Low‑Capital)
1. **Inventory‑Clearance as a Service (ICaaS)** – Monetise surplus stock by offering it to other retailers for a fee.  
2. **Dynamic Pricing Engine (AI‑driven)** – Adjust markdown percentages in real‑time using TikTok‑Shop demand signals.  
3. **Influencer‑Hosted “Shop‑the‑Look” Live Auctions** – Turn clearance into scarcity‑driven auctions; charge a buyer’s premium.  
4. **Loyalty‑Tokenised Clearance Market** – Issue proprietary tokens/points redeemable for clearance items; embed in a gamified loyalty loop.  
5. **Cross‑Brand Concession Co‑Op** – Allow premium third‑party brands to temporarily occupy shelf space for a revenue‑share.

## Cross‑Layer Findings
- **Risk Concentration:** All forward‑looking tactics hinge on influencer‑driven sales; under‑performance jeopardises the 18‑month breakeven target.  
- **Resource Constraints:** Zero new capital forces staff re‑allocation; any digital initiative must be *net‑zero* in labor burden.  
- **Opportunity Leverage:** Non‑obvious expansions (ICaaS, dynamic pricing, token‑clearance, concession co‑op) deliver the biggest cash‑flow upside with lowest operational risk.  
- **Governance Gap:** No formal **SKEPSIS L4** synthesis checkpoint yet; all recommendations should be gated by a L4 synthesis review before execution.
